Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains what information Club Ibiza Tickets collects when you use clubibizatickets.com, and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Contact details you provide directly — name, email, WhatsApp number — when you submit a VIP table request, contact form, or message us.
  • Basic browsing data (pages visited, general location, device type) collected through standard website analytics.

We do not collect payment card details. Ticket purchases are processed entirely through our official partner's secure checkout, which has its own privacy and payment security standards.

How we use it

  • To respond to VIP table requests, booking questions and concierge enquiries.
  • To send booking-related updates via WhatsApp or email when you've requested a service.
  • To understand which pages and guides are useful, so we can improve the site.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing with partners

When you book a ticket, your booking details are shared with our ticketing partner (ClubTickets.com / Only Tickets) as needed to process that purchase. When you request a VIP table, boat experience or activity, relevant details are shared with the specific venue or operator to confirm your reservation.

Cookies

This site may use basic cookies for functionality and anonymous analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work, though some preferences may not be remembered.

Data retention

We keep enquiry and booking-related information only as long as needed to provide the service and meet any legal or accounting requirements, after which it's deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Under EU/Spanish data protection law (GDPR), you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us directly.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, reach out via WhatsApp or mariaibizaespana@gmail.com.
