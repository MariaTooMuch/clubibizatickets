Ambassador Terms

Last updated: August 2026

These terms apply to anyone taking part in the Club Ibiza Tickets Ambassador programme — including personal guide pages such as Bella's Ibiza Guide — in addition to our general Terms & Conditions.

What the programme is

Club Ibiza Tickets works with a small number of Ibiza-based creators and promoters who curate their own recommendations from across our existing clubs, boats and experiences. Each ambassador gets a personal guide page and an internal ambassador code, used only to attribute visits and enquiries that come through that page.

How attribution works

Every ambassador page is tied to a unique internal code (for example BELLA01). When you visit an ambassador's page, that code is stored in your browser for 30 days so that any VIP table enquiry or booking click you make during that window can be correctly attributed to the ambassador who referred you.

This internal code is separate from our ticketing partner's own affiliate tracking (aff=CT184), which remains on every booking link regardless of which ambassador page you arrived from. Ambassador attribution is used for internal recognition and enquiry routing — it does not change pricing, availability or the checkout process, which is always handled directly by our official ticketing partner.

What we don't publish

Commission structures, referral payouts and other internal programme economics are not published on ambassador pages or on this site. Ambassadors receive that information directly from Club Ibiza Tickets.

Ambassador responsibilities

  • Only recommend clubs, events and experiences that are genuinely available and correctly linked through Club Ibiza Tickets or its official partners.
  • Keep pricing, lineup and availability claims accurate — event details can change with little notice, and we're not liable for a promoter's last-minute changes.
  • Represent Club Ibiza Tickets honestly — no misleading claims, fake urgency, or guarantees we haven't made.
  • Respond to enquiries routed to you in a timely, professional manner.

Content and pages

Ambassador guide pages are built and hosted by Club Ibiza Tickets using approved photography, copy and booking links from the main site. Ambassadors do not have direct access to edit their page and should request changes directly.

Ending participation

Either party can end an ambassador's participation in the programme at any time. If ended, the ambassador's guide page and attribution code may be deactivated.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Spain, alongside our general Terms & Conditions.
