Club Ibiza Tickets Ambassador Programme

Club Ibiza Tickets works with a small number of Ibiza-based creators, promoters and hosts who know the island and want to share their own guide to it — clubs, VIP tables, boats and experiences, curated in their own voice and backed by our booking network.

What you get

Your own guide page

A personal, on-brand Ibiza guide built and hosted by us, with your photo, picks and contact details.

Real booking access

Every link routes through our official ticketing partner — the same secure checkout as the main site.

Attributed enquiries

VIP requests and bookings made through your page are tagged to you with a personal ambassador code.

Direct support

A direct line to the Club Ibiza Tickets team for anything your followers or guests need.

Who we're looking for

Creators, promoters and hosts who are genuinely active in Ibiza's nightlife and travel scene, with an audience that trusts their recommendations. We care more about honesty and local knowledge than follower count.

How it works

If we're a fit, we build your guide page using our existing clubs, events, boats and activities, give you a personal ambassador code, and route enquiries from your page straight to you. Programme terms are covered in full in our Ambassador Terms.
