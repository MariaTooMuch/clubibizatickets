About UNVRS

UNVRS is not just another nightclub — it's a paradigm shift in how we experience nightlife. Located in San Rafael on the site of the former Privilege, UNVRS combines cutting-edge technology, world-class sound systems, and immersive visual experiences.

With a capacity of 10,000, UNVRS is one of the largest venues in the world. From AI-driven lighting to immersive 360-degree visuals, every night at UNVRS is a journey.
