Planning Your Ibiza Trip

How many days should I plan for Ibiza?

Three full nights covers the essentials. A week lets you mix big club nights with beach days, a boat trip and Formentera without rushing.

What's the best time of year to visit?

July and August are peak season — full lineups, full energy, full prices. June and September give you the same clubs with smaller crowds and noticeably better rates on flights and villas. Don't overlook the edges of the season either: Opening Season starts in late April, right after IMS Ibiza, when clubs kick off their year with some of the most talked-about parties of the calendar. Closing Season runs September into October, when residencies wrap up with their biggest nights of the year before the island goes quiet for winter.

Should I book everything before I arrive?

Book the big club nights, VIP tables and boat trips in advance — those sell out. Smaller activities and restaurants can usually wait until you're on the island.

Where should I stay?

Playa d'en Bossa puts you close to Hï, Ushuaïa and UNVRS. San Antonio has its own scene and sunset culture. Ibiza Town is more upscale and central for boat trips and dinners.

First Time in Ibiza

What should I know before my first trip?

Nights start later than most places — doors open around midnight, the floor fills after 2am. Plan your dinner and pre-drinks around that, not around a normal night out schedule.

Do I need a rental car?

It helps if you want to explore beyond the main strip, but it's not essential. Taxis get scarce at peak hours and after clubs close — booking transfers in advance removes that stress.

Travelling Solo in Ibiza

Is Ibiza safe for solo travellers?

Yes. It's a major tourist destination with a huge nightlife infrastructure built around visitors. Stick to booked transfers or official taxis late at night and you're set.

Will I feel out of place going to a club alone?

Not really — clubs here are built for groups forming on the dancefloor, not just showing up already in one.

What's the best way to meet people as a solo traveller?

Boat parties and pool parties are the easiest entry point — shared tables, shared music, low pressure, easy to strike up a conversation.

Ibiza for Couples

Is Ibiza good for a couples trip, or is it only for big groups?

Both. Sunset dinners, a private boat trip to Formentera, a VIP table for two at a smaller residency — Ibiza scales down just as well as it scales up.

What's a good club night for a couple who doesn't want an all-night rave?

Look at sunset or early-evening events, or beach-club-style venues instead of the big three mega-clubs — same island, much more relaxed pace.

Ibiza for Groups

What's the easiest way to book for a big group?

Message us directly once you're past 6 tickets on a single order — we handle group rates and coordinate everyone on the same booking.

Should we get a VIP table if we're a group of 8+?

At that size, a table usually works out simpler and often similar in cost per person to standing tickets, with guaranteed space to actually stay together all night.

Ibiza for Families

Are there Ibiza experiences that work for families, not just nightlife?

Yes — daytime boat trips, Formentera day trips and most water sports are built around exactly this. The nightclub side of the island is a separate world from the family-friendly daytime one.

What's the best family day out?

A Formentera day trip is the easiest win — beach time, clear water, a boat ride kids actually enjoy, back in Ibiza by evening.

Choosing the Right Club

What's the difference between UNVRS, Hï, and Ushuaïa?

UNVRS is Ibiza's newest mega-venue and currently the #1 Club in the World by DJ Mag 2026 — massive production, headline residencies across house, techno and open-format nights. Hï Ibiza splits into two rooms (Theatre and Club), letting you move between a polished house/techno night and something rawer without leaving the building. Ushuaïa is open-air by the pool, daytime into evening — the choice when you want sun, spectacle and big commercial energy rather than a late, dark warehouse night.

I don't know much about electronic music — which event is best for me?

Start with the name recognition: if you know the DJ, you'll enjoy the night regardless of sub-genre. If you don't, open-format and commercial house nights are the easiest entry point.

What if I want a quieter, more intimate night instead of a mega-club?

Look at the smaller residencies and beach-club-style venues rather than the three giants — same island, completely different pace.

Music Styles Explained

What's the difference between house, techno and open-format nights?

House leans groove-driven and melodic — the easiest style to dance to for a first-timer. Techno is darker, more repetitive, more hypnotic. Open-format nights mix genres and eras, built around the DJ reading the crowd.

Which clubs play House, Techno or Afro House?

House and tech-house dominate most Ibiza residencies. Techno leans deeper into venues like UNVRS's late-night programming and specific residencies at Hï's Club room. Afro House has grown fast across sunset sets and beach-club-style parties. Check the specific event page — the resident DJs and brand name usually tell you the sound before you even click.

How do I know what style an event plays before booking?

Check the resident DJs and the party brand name — most Ibiza residencies are built around a consistent sound.

Other Ibiza Clubs

Is it only UNVRS, Hï and Ushuaïa, or are there other clubs worth knowing about?

Those three get most of the attention because they're the biggest, but Chinois, Eden, O Beach, Es Paradis, Ibiza Rocks, Lío and more all have their own residencies and crowd. Check the full clubs list on the homepage for the complete picture.

How do I pick between a mega-club and a smaller venue?

Mega-clubs mean production and scale. Smaller venues mean more intimacy and often a more specific music policy — good if you already know exactly what sound you're after.

VIP Tables

How do VIP tables actually work?

You reserve a table for your group at a specific event, which comes with dedicated space, table service and usually a faster way in. Fill out the VIP form with your club, date and group size and we handle the rest.

How far in advance should I book a VIP table?

As early as possible for headline nights — tables at the biggest residencies go fast. A few weeks out is safe for most nights.

Is a VIP table worth it for a smaller group?

Depends on priority — if guaranteed space and skipping the queue matters more than saving money, yes, even for 4-6 people.

Boat Parties

What actually happens on a boat party?

Music, dancing, swimming stops, a crowd that's there for the same energy as a club night — just on the water, with the sun instead of strobe lights.

What should I bring on a boat party?

Swimwear, sunscreen, a towel and a dry bag for your phone. Most parties include drinks — check the specific listing for what's covered.

Is a boat party the same as a boat trip?

No — a boat party is built around music and a party atmosphere. A boat trip is a calmer, scenic day on the water. See Boat Trips below.

Boat Trips

What's the difference between a Boat Trip and a Boat Party?

A boat trip is about the scenery and a relaxed day — swimming stops, snorkelling, lunch on board. A boat party is about the music and the crowd. Pick based on the mood you want.

How long do boat trips usually last?

Most run half a day (4-5 hours) or a full day (7-8 hours), depending on the route and whether Formentera is included.

Do boat trips include food?

Many do — check the specific listing, since this varies by operator and route.

Formentera

What is the best day to visit Formentera?

Any day the sea is calm — but weekdays mean smaller crowds at the best beaches. If you're flexible, avoid peak Saturday boat traffic.

Ferry, cruise or day trip — what's the actual difference?

A ferry gets you there and back, nothing else included — you explore on your own once you land. A cruise or organised day trip adds a boat route and usually lunch, built around not having to plan anything yourself.

Do I need to book Formentera in advance?

In peak season, yes — the popular morning ferries and boat trips sell out days ahead.

Activities & Experiences

What activities are available beyond the clubs and beach?

Buggy tours, quad adventures, hot air balloon rides, market visits and more — the island has a full daytime side most people never plan for.

How do I know what's worth doing versus a tourist trap?

Message us — this is exactly the kind of local knowledge that's faster to ask than to guess from reviews.

Water Sports

What water sports are available in Ibiza?

Jet skis, eFoils, e-surf boards and more, mostly based out of San Antonio and Playa d'en Bossa.

Do I need experience to try an eFoil or e-surf?

No — most sessions include a quick briefing and are built for first-timers.

Tickets & Payments

Do I need to print my ticket?

No — your e-ticket and QR code work straight from your phone. Just make sure you have signal or a screenshot saved for the door.

Is it cheaper to buy online than at the door?

Yes, in almost every case. Online tickets typically run €10-15 below door price, and that gap widens as an event gets closer to selling out.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa and Mastercard, plus PayPal, protected by 3D Secure authentication.

Is there a limit on how many tickets I can buy at once?

Up to 6 per order for security reasons. Coming as a bigger group? Message us and we'll sort the rest.

Cancellation & Refunds

Can I cancel my booking?

Only if you added Cancellation Insurance 360 at checkout (from €2.99), which gives a full refund with at least 24 hours' notice. Without it, tickets are final sale, same as buying at the door.

What happens if the event is cancelled by the venue?

You're covered regardless of insurance — we'll reach out with instructions as soon as we're informed.

What if the lineup or DJ changes after I've bought my ticket?

Lineup changes are part of how Ibiza's big residencies work. A DJ swap alone doesn't trigger a refund unless it counts as a major change to the whole event.

Transport & Getting Around

Is there a free way to get around at night?

Yes — the Disco Bus. It is a free night bus network connecting Ibiza Town, Playa d'en Bossa, San Antonio and the main clubs through the night, running on a set schedule during the season. It is reliable, but it works on its own timetable and gets crowded at peak hours — for a specific event or a tight schedule, a booked transfer is still the safer bet.

How do I get around Ibiza without a car?

Taxis, buses between main towns, and pre-booked transfers for club nights. Taxis get scarce at peak hours and after clubs close, so booking transfers for the nights that matter removes the stress entirely.

How do I get back after a club closes at 6am?

Book a transfer in advance for exactly this — trying to find a taxi outside a mega-club at closing time is the single most common frustration we hear about.

Is it worth renting a car?

Only if you want to explore beyond the main strip on your own schedule. For a nightlife-focused trip, transfers are simpler.

Dress Code

What should I wear to an Ibiza club?

Smart casual to genuinely dressy, depending on the venue — think elevated resort wear, not beachwear. Skip flip-flops, swimwear and full sportswear.

Can I bring a backpack or camera?

Most venues restrict large bags and professional cameras at the door for security reasons. Keep it to a small bag or crossbody.

Is there somewhere to store my things during the night?

Yes — Hï Ibiza, Ushuaïa and UNVRS all have lockers available on-site, so you can leave a jacket, bag or camera safely instead of carrying it around all night. Availability is first-come, first-served, so it is worth locating them early if you know you will need one.

Safety

Is Ibiza safe overall?

Yes, it's a well-established tourist destination. Standard precautions apply: official taxis or booked transfers at night, and keep your ticket and ID somewhere secure.

What should I watch out for at clubs specifically?

Keep your drink with you at all times, same as any big venue anywhere, and know your transfer plan home before the night starts, not after.

Accessibility

Are Ibiza's clubs accessible for visitors with mobility needs?

It varies by venue — message us with the specific club and event, and we'll confirm accessibility details before you book.

Can I request accessibility accommodations for boat trips or activities?

Yes, message us ahead of booking so we can check with the specific operator.
